### About

I am a researcher and educator working on natural language processing for low-resource African languages, with a focus on Hausa. My doctoral research at King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), Bangkok, is supported by the Petchra Pra Jom Klao Ph.D. Research Scholarship.

I am also an Assistant Lecturer at Northwest University, Kano, where I teach undergraduate courses in Artificial Intelligence.

### Research Interests

- **Low-resource NLP** for Hausa and other African languages
- **Speech recognition** with Boko (Latin) and Ajami (Arabic-script) Hausa
- **Computational humour** generation, evaluation and LLM-as-a-judge methods
